DeskBuddy - 1 minute pitch

Hi, I'm Antares, and my project is DeskBuddy.

So this happens to me a lot: I sit down to study in the afternoon, and suddenly it's dark and I'm squinting at my laptop. Or it's Seattle in November and I only notice my room is freezing when my hands are already cold.

DeskBuddy is a little box that sits on my desk and notices these things for me.

It has two sensors on top. A photoresistor checks how bright my desk is, and when it gets too dark, a yellow LED turns on to remind me to turn on my lamp. Once the lamp is on, the light goes off by itself.

A thermistor checks the room temperature. When it gets too cold, it beeps once and a blue LED stays on until the room warms up again.

Inside it's just an Arduino and a breadboard in a cardboard box, so the main work is calibrating the two sensors and building the box. My timeline and backup plans are on the slide.

That's DeskBuddy. Thanks!
